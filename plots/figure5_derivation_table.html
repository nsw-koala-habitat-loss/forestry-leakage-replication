BACI = (After, treated - Before, treated) - (After, control - Before, control); a cell is 625 m^2 (25 x 25 m).
Matching QC: parallel pre-trends (p >= 0.05), SMD (< 0.25), proportion of treated units retained (>= 0.8), on the model-selected matching spec. 'All 3 met' = meets_criteria; '--' where no matchit summary exists for that run-year.
Step 3 uses the INLA model's average marginal effect (accounts for matching covariates), not the raw BACI count directly. It scales the matched-sample AME to the full leakage zone by woody_veg_leakage_zone_ha / (new protected area); per-year bounds are quantiles of delta_area x that positive constant ratio.
Pooled rows: per-year delta_area draws scaled to the leakage zone (Step 3), then within each of 10,000 posterior draws summed across years (Step 3) or combined as sum(leakage)/sum(avoided clearing) (Step 4 aggregate); 2.5/50/97.5% quantiles are then taken over draws. New protected area and avoided clearing on pooled rows are summed over the pooled years. 'Pooled QC (n)' keeps only the n years meeting all 3 matching-QC criteria and is the pooling reported in Figure 5; 'Pooled all (n)' uses every fitted year. The pooled Multiplier is an aggregate ratio, not the pooled median effect divided by a clearing value.
